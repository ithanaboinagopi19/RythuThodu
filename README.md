# RythuThodu 🌾

### Smart Crop Care and Direct Market Access for Farmers

RythuThodu is a multilingual farmer-support platform designed to assist farmers throughout the crop journey — from identifying crop diseases and receiving treatment guidance to checking market prices, connecting with buyers and FPOs, finding cold-storage facilities, and arranging transportation.

The platform focuses on providing simple, farmer-friendly information for users with limited digital literacy, regional-language requirements, and unreliable internet connectivity.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Small and marginal farmers face challenges not only during crop cultivation but also after harvesting. Identifying crop diseases, understanding suitable treatment, finding better market opportunities, locating storage facilities, and arranging transportation often require different sources and intermediaries.

RythuThodu brings these activities into a single platform to support farmers in making better crop-care and selling decisions.

---

## 💡 Solution

RythuThodu provides an integrated workflow:

**Crop Image → Disease/Pest Diagnosis → Treatment Guidance → Market Prices → Buyers/FPOs → Cold Storage → Logistics**

Instead of treating crop care and selling as separate activities, the platform connects them into one continuous farmer journey.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🌱 Crop Disease Detection
- Select the crop and submit a crop image.
- Generate a structured crop-health diagnosis.
- Display possible disease/pest information.
- Show symptoms, severity/confidence information and quick actions.

### 🌿 Treatment Guidance
Treatment information is organized into:
- Cultural practices
- Biological control
- Chemical control
- Prevention
- Safety guidance

### 📊 Market Prices
- View crop-related market-price information.
- Filter available market records.
- Help farmers compare available market opportunities before selling.

### 🤝 Buyers & FPOs
- Discover potential buyers and Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs).
- View buyer/FPO information.
- Create produce listings.
- Submit selling offers.

### ❄️ Cold Storage
- Discover available cold-storage options.
- View storage information.
- Submit storage booking requests.

### 🚚 Logistics
- Provide transportation requirements.
- Submit pickup and destination details.
- Create transport/logistics requests for moving produce.

### 🌐 Multilingual Support

The platform supports:

- English
- Telugu
- Hindi
- Tamil
- Kannada
- Marathi

The localization system is designed to make the application easier to use for regional-language farmers.

### 📶 Connectivity Support
- Detect online/offline network state.
- Preserve supported application data locally.
- Provide a connectivity-aware user experience for areas with unreliable internet.
